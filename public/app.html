NUT-00 BDHKE Blind Signature Cryptography

Bellare-Goldwasser Blind Diffie-Hellman Key Exchange over secp256k1 elliptic curve ensuring untraceable cash tokens.

1. Client Blinding Protocol

Client picks random secret x, computes Y = hash_to_curve(x). Picks secret blinding factor r.

B_ = Y + r * G (mod p)

The mint signs B_ without learning Y or x. Privacy is mathematically guaranteed.

2. Mint Signing & Unblinding

Mint signs with private key k: C_ = k * B_. Client unblinds using public mint key K = k * G:

C = C_ - r * K = k * Y

Final pair (x, C) is a valid, bearer Chaumian token spendable at any DVM worker.
